# Cedric Laier

Prioritizing user needs, team empowerment, and sustainable engineering. 🌱

I’ve built software for over a decade and help teams do their best work. I’m interested in the details that make a product useful in practice: understanding the problem, choosing a sensible approach, and leaving behind something people can keep working on.

My last tenure was with Yara International: eight years leading 15+ engineers across three teams, building digital products including **Atfarm**, **YaraPlus**, **YaraConnect**, **YaraIrix**, and **CoffeeClub**. The work ranged from crop nutrition software to commercial tools, and included starting a pilot to connect Salesforce with Yara’s digital offerings. I’m continuing in plant nutrition now.

## How I work

I’m an introvert, and I listen before reaching for an answer. I want people to be able to say “I don’t know”, point out when something is broken, and question a decision, including mine. Good leadership, to me, means giving people context and direction, then trusting them with the work.

I stay close to the code. That helps keep technical decisions tied to the needs of the product and the people who will maintain it. I see every framework and language as a tool in an ever-growing toolbox, not a trend to follow. I work across modern (but also legacy) systems and pick what fits the problem rather than the other way around. Shipping matters; so does making the next change possible.
